Meet Roberta

I did not become someone new.
I remembered who I was.

And this work grew from the journey back to myself.

For years, I was the woman who looked like she had everything together.

I built a successful corporate career, became the person people relied on, and kept moving forward even when something inside me had gone quiet.

Leaving that life was not one dramatic leap. It was a series of honest choices, made slowly enough for my body and my life to catch up.

Blooming Soul Journeys was born from that process and from the belief that insight matters most when it changes how we live.

Today, I support women and leaders through coaching that blends honest reflection, embodiment, nervous-system awareness, systemic work and grounded action.
